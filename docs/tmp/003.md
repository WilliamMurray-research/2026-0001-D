**canonical taxonomy of governance domains** — not the corporate fluff, not the ISO‑9001 boilerplate, but the structurally meaningful categories that matter inside a governed architecture like yours.

## 🧱 The 12 Core Governance Domains  
These are the domains that appear across every mature governance system — political, organisational, technical, ecological, or hybrid. They’re the *irreducible set*.

### 1. **Strategic Governance**  
Direction‑setting, purpose, mandate, long‑range intent.  
Includes: strategy, mission, objectives, stewardship.

### 2. **Structural Governance**  
How the system is arranged: roles, authorities, boundaries, interfaces.  
Includes: organisational design, domain boundaries, custodial structures.

### 3. **Operational Governance**  
Execution rules for day‑to‑day functioning.  
Includes: procedures, workflows, operational constraints.

### 4. **Risk Governance**  
Identification, evaluation, mitigation, and monitoring of risk.  
Includes: risk frameworks, controls, tolerances, escalation paths.

### 5. **Compliance & Regulatory Governance**  
Ensuring alignment with external obligations.  
Includes: legal compliance, standards, audits, certifications.

### 6. **Security Governance**  
Protection of assets, information, systems, and people.  
Includes: cyber, physical, personnel, threat modelling.

### 7. **Information Governance**  
Custody, classification, flow, and lifecycle of information.  
Includes: data models, metadata, retention, access control.

### 8. **Technical Governance**  
Rules for systems, architecture, engineering, and technology choices.  
Includes: architecture standards, design principles, versioning, interfaces.

### 9. **Financial Governance**  
Resource allocation, budgeting, cost control, capital management.  
Includes: funding rules, approvals, financial risk.

### 10. **Ethical Governance**  
Norms, values, constraints, and moral boundaries.  
Includes: fairness, harm minimisation, rights, duty‑of‑care.

### 11. **People & Capability Governance**  
Competency, roles, training, authority, performance.  
Includes: skill frameworks, role definitions, delegation.

### 12. **Environmental & Sustainability Governance**  
Ecological impact, resource use, long‑term viability.  
Includes: sustainability principles, ecological constraints.
